Client Success Case Studies

Client Success Case Study 01

Building Trust During a High-Risk Onboarding

Context & Challenge

A newly onboarded client expressed concerns about our ability to deliver and was considering leaving early in the relationship. The main issue wasn't simply execution—it was a lack of confidence and visibility into how we worked. I needed to rebuild trust while keeping the onboarding moving forward.

My Approach

I made the account a top priority and created a structured weekly plan covering key actions and milestones. I established a weekly call to maintain alignment, review progress, and give the client a consistent point of contact.

Outcome

Over the following weeks, the client became more comfortable with our processes and developed a better understanding of how we worked, leading to a successful onboarding.
