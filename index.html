CASE STUDY 01

Building Trust During a High-Risk Onboarding

Challenge

A newly onboarded client had lost confidence in the process and was considering leaving before implementation was complete.

Approach

I made the account a top priority, created a structured weekly plan, established recurring calls, and ensured the client received written updates whenever they could not attend.

Outcome

The client gained confidence in our process, understood how we worked, and successfully moved through onboarding.

Turning a Support Issue Into a Cross-Functional Action Plan

Challenge

An existing client became frustrated after experiencing delays with support and began questioning whether their needs were receiving enough attention.

Approach
