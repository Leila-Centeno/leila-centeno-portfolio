Client Success Case Studies

Client Success Case Study 01

Building Trust During a High-Risk Onboarding

Context & Challenge

A newly onboarded client expressed concerns about our ability to deliver and was considering leaving early in the relationship. The main issue wasn't simply execution—it was a lack of confidence and visibility into how we worked. I needed to rebuild trust while keeping the onboarding moving forward.

My Approach

I made the account a top priority and created a structured weekly plan covering key actions and milestones. I established a weekly call to maintain alignment, review progress, and give the client a consistent point of contact. When they couldn't attend, I sent clear weekly updates so they always knew what was completed, what was next, and where things stood—focusing on consistency and predictability over time.

Outcome

Over the following weeks, the client became more comfortable with our processes and developed a better understanding of how we worked. The relationship stabilized as we continued progressing toward a successful onboarding.

Client Success Case Study 02

Turning a Support Issue Into a Cross-Functional Action Plan

Context & Challenge

An existing client was becoming frustrated because their support requests were not being addressed within the expected timeframe. The delays were affecting the client's experience and creating a potential retention risk. I needed to understand internal issues while ensuring the client felt heard and supported.

My Approach

I raised the issue during a retention-focused conversation with the client to understand the exact business impact. I then worked directly with internal teams involved, communicated the urgency, and made the account a priority. I also joined calls with the client when additional coordination was needed, keeping both sides aligned and keeping the client informed about progress.

Outcome

The issue received increased internal attention and the client had a dedicated point of contact to help move outstanding requests forward. This helped rebuild confidence and strengthen the client relationship.
