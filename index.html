Case Study 01

Building Trust During a High-Risk Onboarding

Context & Challenge

A newly onboarded client expressed concerns about our ability to deliver and was considering leaving early in the relationship. The main issue was a lack of confidence and visibility into how we worked. I needed to rebuild trust while keeping the onboarding moving forward.

My Approach

I made the account a top priority and created a structured weekly plan covering key actions and milestones. I established a weekly call to maintain alignment and review progress. When they couldn't attend, I provided clear written updates so they always knew what was completed, what was next, and where things stood—focusing on consistency and predictability over time.

Outcome

The client developed trust in our processes and team, stabilizing the relationship and continuing smoothly toward a successful onboarding.

Case Study 02

Turning a Support Issue Into a Cross-Functional Action Plan

Context & Challenge

An existing client was frustrated because support requests were not being addressed within the expected timeframe, creating a potential retention risk. I needed to understand internal delays while ensuring the client felt heard and supported.

My Approach

I raised the issue during a retention-focused conversation with the client to understand the exact business impact. I then collaborated directly with internal support teams, communicated the urgency, prioritized the account, and joined coordination calls to bridge the gap between internal teams and the client.

Outcome

The issue received immediate internal focus, and providing the client with a dedicated point of contact rebuilt their confidence and strengthened the long-term relationship.
