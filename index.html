Case Studies

Examples of how I approach client risk, communication, prioritization, and cross-functional delivery.

CASE STUDY 01

Building Trust During a High-Risk Onboarding

Challenge

A newly onboarded client had lost confidence in the process and was considering leaving before the implementation was complete.

Approach

I made the account a top priority, created a structured weekly plan, established recurring calls, and made sure the client received a written weekly update whenever they could not attend.

Outcome

Over time, the client gained confidence in our process, understood how we worked, and successfully moved through onboarding without leaving.

CASE STUDY 02

Turning a Support Issue Into a Cross-Functional Action Plan

Challenge

An existing client became frustrated after experiencing delays with support and started questioning whether their needs were receiving enough attention.

Approach

I coordinated with the relevant internal teams, communicated the urgency clearly, prioritized the account, and kept the client informed throughout the resolution process.

Outcome

The issue received increased internal attention, communication with the client improved, and the relationship became stronger through proactive ownership of the escalation.
